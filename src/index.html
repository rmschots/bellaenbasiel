Bella & Basiel — Bed & Breakfast in Bruges

Bella & Basiel is a family-run bed & breakfast in the quiet, green Saint Ann district of Bruges (Brugge), Belgium — inside the old city walls and a ten-minute walk from the Markt and the historic centre.

Three cosy guest rooms

Each room has a comfortable 1.80 m boxspring bed, a modern private bathroom with walk-in shower, free Wi-Fi and TV. Room 1 (2 persons, €114/night), Room 2 (2–3 persons with optional sofa bed, €125/night) and Room 3 (2 persons, €104/night). Minimum stay 2 nights. Optional breakfast (€20 pp) is served in the dining room or the sunny garden. Garage parking available (€20/night). Free cot and high chair for families.

Frequently asked questions

Is parking available?
Yes, garage parking for €20 per night. Loading and unloading in front of the house; the hosts help you find parking.
Are pets allowed?
No — the hosts have their own dog (who loves guests).
Is breakfast included?
Breakfast is optional at €20 per person per day, served in the dining room or the sunny garden.
Is there a minimum stay?
Yes, 2 nights. Prices exclude tourist tax (€4.50 per person per night). Discounts: −10% for all 3 rooms or −10% for 7 nights (not cumulative).
Is it suitable for young children?
Yes, a cot and high chair are available free of charge. Room 2 has an optional sofa bed for a third guest (€27 pppn).
How far is the centre of Bruges?
A ten-minute walk from the Markt, in the quiet Saint Ann (Sint-Anna) district inside the old city walls.
How do I get there from the station?
Bus 2 from platform A1 (exit "Centrum"), or a taxi (±€12–16).
Is smoking allowed?
In the garden only.
How do I book?
